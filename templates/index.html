The organizing principle

Two levels of “application”

An application is a decision or scientific use. A computational problem is the task an algorithm actually solves. Linking the two keeps a speedup theorem from being mistaken for a product.

Case 01 · Classical baseline

Vehicle option-package pricing

The published nine-variable input has an exact sorting solution. We reproduced its 827×345 DQI encoding and compiled a 45-qubit arithmetic Grover circuit for the original decision problem. The result narrows the claim for this instance; richer pricing models remain open.
